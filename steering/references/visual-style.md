# Visual style: mind and spirit

The look: dark and warm, but vivid, high-contrast and always moving. Cinematic, never sleepy.
How fast to cut (a new picture every 3 to 5 seconds) is set in `steering/instructions.md`.
This file covers what to search for.

## Footage searches
Write each scene's visual as a 2 to 4 word stock-footage search, with the concrete subject
first (the subject word must appear in the clip's tags). Add one motion word when it helps
(timelapse, flowing, swirling, rising, falling, flythrough). Pick what matches the meaning of
the sentence.

| When the narration is about | Search for |
|---|---|
| the mind, thoughts, attention | ink water, smoke swirl, neurons firing, light trails |
| the universe, the infinite, purpose | galaxy timelapse, milky way timelapse, aurora timelapse, nebula flythrough |
| old wisdom, the past, a book | book pages turning, candle flame, quill writing, light rays dust |
| stillness, meditation, presence | sunrise timelapse, mist rolling, meditation silhouette, lotus flower |
| time, change, habits | hourglass sand, clock gears, clouds timelapse, city timelapse |
| struggle, fear, rumination | storm lightning, storm clouds timelapse, rain window, fog moving |
| growth, hope, a new start | sunrise mountains, seedling timelapse, forest sunlight, dawn timelapse |
| the self, identity, choice | mirror reflection, crossroads aerial, door light, footprints sand |

## Rules
- Prefer clips with visible movement, strong light and rich contrast over still, dim or washed-out
  ones. Calm subjects are fine if something in the frame moves (light, mist, water, clouds).
- A still, quiet shot is allowed only as a deliberate pause, on a [pause], a cliffhanger or a
  book quote.
- No animals unless the script is literally about one.
- No close-ups of people talking to camera (they look like adverts); silhouettes, hands and
  people seen from behind are fine.
- No symbols of one specific faith (including temples) unless the topic is that faith.
- Long videos use landscape footage only.
- Never use rapid flashing or flickering (more than 3 flashes per second).
- Concept images (when enabled): painterly or cinematic scenes, never realistic portraits of real
  people.
