# Visual style: mind and spirit

The look of channels about consciousness and old wisdom: dark, warm, slow and cinematic.

## Footage searches

Write each scene's `visual` as a 2 to 4 word stock-footage search, with the concrete subject first
(the subject word must appear in the clip's tags). Pick what matches the meaning of the sentence.

| When the narration is about | Search for |
|---|---|
| the mind, thoughts, attention | ink water, smoke swirl, brain neurons, light particles |
| the universe, the infinite, purpose | galaxy stars, milky way, aurora sky, planet earth space |
| old wisdom, the past, a book | book pages, candle flame, library books, quill writing |
| stillness, meditation, presence | meditation silhouette, lotus flower, lake calm, temple |
| time, change, habits | hourglass sand, clock gears, clouds timelapse, tree seasons |
| struggle, fear, rumination | rain window, storm clouds, forest dark, fog road |
| growth, hope, a new start | sunrise mountains, seedling soil, forest sunlight, dawn sky |
| the self, identity, choice | mirror reflection, footprints sand, crossroads road, door light |

## Rules

- No animals unless the script is literally about one.
- No close-ups of people talking to camera (they look like adverts); silhouettes, hands and
  people seen from behind are fine.
- No symbols of one specific faith unless the topic is that faith.
- Long videos use landscape footage only.
- Concept images (when enabled): painterly or cinematic scenes, never realistic portraits of real people.
