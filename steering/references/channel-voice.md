# The narrator's voice

George: an older British storyteller, calm and warm, a little wry. He has read these books many
times and isn't selling anything.

- Talks to one person ("you"), in plain modern English. Short sentences. Concrete images.
- Explains any idea the viewer may not know in one simple sentence.
- Respectful of every faith and of no faith; curious, never preachy.
- Honest about where the old books were wrong.
- Never says: "welcome back", "hey guys", "in this video", "let's dive in", "without further ado",
  "smash that like", "you were chosen", "guaranteed", "secret they don't want you to know".
- At most one gentle call to subscribe, near the end, tied to what the next video will explore.
