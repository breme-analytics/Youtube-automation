# YouTube guardrails

Checked against YouTube's monetization and spam policies in October 2026. These come before style.

1. **Transform, don't read.** YouTube doesn't pay for videos that only read other people's text.
   Every video explains, questions and tests the book; quotes are short and checked word for word.
2. **Not mass-produced.** Each video has its own argument, structure and examples. The owner
   reviews every video before it's posted.
3. **Titles, thumbnails and descriptions match the video.** Misleading metadata breaks YouTube's
   spam policy.
4. **No expert persona on sensitive topics.** The narrator never claims to be a doctor, therapist,
   financial adviser or lawyer, and gives no medical, legal or money advice. When the old books make
   health claims, say what science found and suggest seeing a professional for real problems.
5. **Shock for its own sake isn't allowed.** Negativity in a title is fine when the video answers it;
   gratuitous fear, despair or disturbing material is not.
6. **AI disclosure.** The narration is an AI voice and is labelled in the description. Never show
   realistic AI images of real people or real events.
7. **Rights.** Only public-domain books (verified sources), Pixabay footage (credited), our own
   images and narration. Living teachers may be discussed and credited, but never their recordings,
   footage, or more than a brief attributed phrase of their text.
8. **Advertiser-friendly.** No profanity, graphic content or mockery of anyone's faith.
9. **Report studies honestly.** One study "suggests" or "found"; it never "proves". Use the study's own
   numbers and wording, say when it was small or early, and never turn a link ("linked to") into a cause
   ("drives", "rewires", "shields").
10. **Old metaphysics is belief, not science.** Books like *The Kybalion* make claims about vibration,
   polarity or a universal mind. Say what the authors believed and why it still speaks to people, then
   what psychology actually supports. Never present it as physics, and never borrow scientific words
   ("quantum", "frequency", "energy field") to make it sound proven. Treat every faith and tradition
   with respect. (Added 13 Oct 2026, with the new books.)
