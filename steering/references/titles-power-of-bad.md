# Titles and thumbnails: the negativity effect

Source: John Tierney and Roy F. Baumeister, *The Power of Bad: How the Negativity Effect Rules Us
and How We Can Rule It* (Penguin Press, 2019). Summarised in our own words.

## What the book says, briefly

- **Bad is stronger than good.** Negative events, feelings and feedback affect people more strongly
  than positive ones of the same size. This is the negativity effect (or negativity bias).
- **The rule of four.** As a rough average, it takes about four good things to outweigh one bad one.
- **Losses loom larger than gains.** People work harder to avoid losing something than to gain the
  same thing.
- **Bad news travels further.** Threats and warnings grab attention, which is why media and
  marketers lean on them. The authors warn that constant fearmongering distorts how people see
  the world.
- **The way out is to use the bias on purpose:** let the bad get attention and teach, then let the
  good win.

## Rules for titles

1. Frame the title around a **cost, a mistake, a hidden threat, a loss or a warning**: something
   the viewer is losing, getting wrong, or not seeing.
2. **Keep it true.** The video must confront that threat in its first 30 seconds and resolve it by
   the end. A title that tricks people breaks YouTube's rules on misleading metadata and leaves
   viewers unsatisfied.
3. **Specific beats vague.** Name the thing (a year, a book, a habit, a feeling), not "This will
   change your life".
4. Aim for **60 characters or fewer** (phones cut long titles). No all-caps sentences; at most
   one word in capitals.
5. **No fear you can't back up:** no health scares, no money scares, no "you were chosen", no doom.
6. **The rule of four inside the video:** answer the title's one "bad" with at least four "goods"
   (an insight, evidence, a practice, hope) before the end.

## Title patterns that fit the channel

- The ___ mistake that keeps you ___
- Why your mind keeps ___ (a 1903 answer)
- Stop ___ before it ___
- What ___ is quietly costing you
- ___ was wrong about ___ (and right about something worse)
- The dark side of ___
- You're not ___, you're ___

## Thumbnail text

Two to four words that add to the title rather than repeat it, with one negative trigger word in
the accent colour (STOP, WRONG, COSTS, NEVER, HIDDEN).
